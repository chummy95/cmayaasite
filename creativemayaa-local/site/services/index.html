Frequently Asked Questions

What sets Creative Maya Studio apart?

We don’t just make things look good, we build magnetic brands with strategy at the core. Every choice we make is intentional, every design is backed by research, and every client walks away with a brand that feels like them and attracts their dream audience.

Why should I invest in branding instead of using Canva or a cheap designer?

Because strategy-led branding saves you from looking generic, attracts premium clients, and sets you up for long-term success—not just a quick fix.

How do I know if I’m ready for branding?

If you feel like your visuals don’t reflect the quality of your work, if you’re struggling to stand out, or if your business has grown beyond the DIY stage, you’re ready. Branding isn’t about size… it’s about clarity and confidence.

Do you work with clients globally?

Yes, we work with clients globally. Regardless of your location, we’re equipped to collaborate effectively and get the work done, no matter where you are.

Can you design a website without branding?

We believe design works best when there’s a strong foundation. That’s why we always recommend starting with brand strategy and identity. A website without branding is just a template. A branded website is a powerful sales tool.
