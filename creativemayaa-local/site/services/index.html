Questions

Before you book

Anything else on your mind? Ask us on your Walkthrough Call.

Why are your prices “from”?

Every business is different. Your final investment depends on scope, such as the number of website pages, products or extra assets. You’ll receive a fixed quote after your Walkthrough Call, before anything begins, so there are no surprises.

What do I need to get started?

A clear idea of your business and where it’s heading, and ideally a settled business name. You don’t need everything figured out — that’s what the strategy phase is for.

What if I don’t know exactly what I want?

That’s completely normal. Our strategy phase is designed to uncover it: through the questionnaire, research and strategy session, we shape a clear direction together before any design begins.

Which platforms do you build websites on?

We design and build on Shopify, WordPress and Squarespace, chosen around how you’ll run your business day to day. For founders who want something fully bespoke, we also hand-build custom-coded websites from scratch — no templates, no page-builder limits, fast and secure by design. We’ll recommend the right route on your Walkthrough Call.

Can you design a website without branding?

Design works best on a strong foundation, so we always recommend starting with brand strategy and identity. A website without a brand behind it is just a template; a branded website is a powerful sales tool.

What isn’t included in the price?

Third-party costs such as font and stock image licences, printing, hosting, domains and paid apps or plugins. Copywriting isn’t included unless agreed in your proposal. We’ll flag any of these before we start.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit secures your place in our calendar, with the remaining balance due as set out in your proposal. Payment plans are available on request.

Do you work with clients globally?

Yes. We work with founders across the UK, the Middle East and beyond, and our process is built to run smoothly wherever you are.
