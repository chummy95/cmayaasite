How it started

It started on a random night in May 2024.

Before starting Creative Mayaa Studio, I worked as a data analyst for two years. But my love for design never really went away. What started as a creative outlet slowly became something I wanted to pursue full-time — so on a random night in the middle of May 2024, I decided to go for it.

Since then, I’ve wholeheartedly enjoyed helping business owners bring their ideas to life through strategic branding and web design. I’ve had the privilege of working with incredible clients across multiple continents, learning more than I ever imagined and building a business that fulfils me deeply.

I have enormous faith in what I’m building — and the plan is to take you all along with me. One project, post or email at a time.
