Answering Questions You May Have

What happens after I order?

Once you complete your purchase, you’ll receive a quick form to personalize your brand kit (business name, tagline, etc.).
From there, we will customize your kit and deliver your full brand suite in 3-5 business days.

What files will I receive?
  • Logo suite (primary, secondary, submark)
  • Custom color palette
  • Brand pattern
  • Custom typography selection
  • Brand guidelines (Canva and Adobe compatible)
Will I get additional materials?

Your purchase includes everything listed on the product page (logo suite, color palette, font recommendations, and a mini brand guide).
If you’d like to add custom brand assets like social templates, business cards, or packaging—we offer those as add-ons. You’ll see the options after checkout.

How many revisions are included?

One revision round is included in the package. Any additional refinements will be billed at $50 per hour.

Do you offer refunds?

Due to the digital nature of the product, no refunds are available. However, we always work to ensure you’re thrilled with the final result.
