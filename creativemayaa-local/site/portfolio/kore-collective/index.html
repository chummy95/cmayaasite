Kore Collective

About The Brand

Kore Collective is a women’s health and wellness practice based in Bangkok, Thailand, founded by Taissa, a naturopath, functional medicine practitioner, and nutritional therapist specialising in fertility and hormonal health.

Rooted in a holistic, science-informed approach, Kore Collective was created to help women move beyond symptom management and better understand their bodies through personalised, root-cause care.

Our Approach

Our process began with brand strategy, where we worked closely with the client to define the brand’s purpose, values, positioning, and target audience. This also included collaborating on the brand name, Kore Collective, to ensure it aligned with the vision and direction of the business.

The Transformation

With a clear strategy in place, we developed a visual identity that feels warm, natural, and professional. We combined a rich, earthy colour palette with elegant typography, custom botanical illustrations, and thoughtful design elements to reflect the brand’s holistic approach to women’s health.

The result is a cohesive and distinctive brand identity that communicates trust, care, and expertise while creating a strong foundation for the brand's growth.

Working with Creative Mayaa Studio on the visual identity for my brand was an absolute pleasure. From the very beginning, she took the time to truly understand my vision, values, and the feeling I wanted my brand to evoke.

The final visual identity exceeded my expectations. Every element was thoughtfully designed and felt aligned with the essence of my brand, elegant, sophisticated, and distinctive. She has an incredible ability to translate ideas and emotions into beautiful, cohesive design.

I couldn’t be happier with the outcome and would highly recommend Creative Mayaa Studio to anyone looking for a designer who combines creativity, strategy, and exceptional attention to detail.

Thank you for helping bring my vision to life so beautifully.

TAISSA OLIVIERA - NATUROPATH & FOUNDER OF KORE COLLECTIVE
